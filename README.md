# 💳 Andhra Bank – Customer Spend Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze credit/debit card customer spending patterns, demographic distributions, and transaction behaviors for Andhra Bank. This project helps business teams identify high-value customer segments, optimize promotional campaigns, and understand month-on-month spend trends.

---

## 📌 Business Objectives
- **Demographic Profiling:** Understand spending habits across different age groups, genders, and professions.
- **Category & Channel Analysis:** Identify top spending categories (e.g., Groceries, Fuel, Travel, Electronics, Dining).
- **City & Regional Performance:** Pinpoint high-performing cities and districts driving maximum transaction volumes.
- **Card Performance:** Evaluate spend volume across different card types (Silver, Gold, Platinum).

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query (Data cleaning, handling nulls, unpivoting, type conversions)
- **Calculations & Logic:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- **Data Source:** Excel / CSV transaction and customer demographic dataset

---

## 📐 Key DAX Measures & KPIs Built
- **Total Spend Amount:** Sum of all customer transactions.
- **Average Spend per Customer:** Evaluating transaction depth per account.
- **Transaction Volume:** Count of successful card swipes/purchases.
- **Spend Growth MoM (%):** Tracking month-on-month changes in spending behavior.
- **Category Contribution (%):** Share of individual merchant categories against overall expenditure.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights
1. **Top Spending Demographics:** Salaried individuals in the 26–40 age bracket contribute the largest share of discretionary spending.
2. **Category Distribution:** Bills, utilities, and grocery purchases provide steady transaction volume, while electronics and travel drive high-ticket value spikes.
3. **Regional Spread:** Tier-1 and major urban clusters show significantly higher card penetration and average ticket sizes.
